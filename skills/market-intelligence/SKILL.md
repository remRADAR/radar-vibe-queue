# Market Intelligence

Produce research and risk analysis, not guaranteed investment advice.

1. Resolve the entity, ticker/security, exchange/listing, currency, and analysis period.
2. Prefer primary filings and official sources for decision-relevant facts. Cite links and publication/filing dates. Timestamp market data; distinguish live/session, delayed, and historical values.
3. Keep fiscal periods, units, and data cut-off explicit. Separate reported facts, calculations, estimates, and interpretation; reconcile figures to source documents.
4. Do not fabricate prices, filings, news, estimates, or hidden facts. State missing data and uncertainty.
5. For portfolio risk, assess concentration, correlated exposures, liquidity, volatility/drawdown, and scenarios rather than relying on one metric.
6. Never guarantee returns or represent the analysis as licensed professional advice. Never expose credentials or execute trades/broker actions without exact authorization and verified tool support. Obtain required confirmation before consequential financial actions.

Deliver thesis, evidence, counterpoints/risks, assumptions, period/data cut-off, source list, and limitations. Mark unverifiable claims as unverified.

# Authorized Product Study

Establish ownership/permission and permitted target/scope first. If unclear, limit analysis to publicly observable behavior and user-provided materials. Map visible features, user flows, responsive behavior, content organization, and public integrations; record evidence and uncertainty. Do not present hidden architecture guesses as facts.

Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, rate limits, or access controls; extract private data, credentials, protected code, or restricted assets; or copy proprietary branding, text, or assets without permission. Distinguish functional inspiration from protected expression and implement an independent equivalent when requested.

Deliver a feature/flow map, evidence and confidence, usability/accessibility observations, risks/unknowns, and scoped implementation plan. When building, use the application/web skill and verify the result separately.

# RADARMeta Following Hygiene

Use only when the authorized connector exposes a legitimate following list and enough relationship evidence. The purpose is to maintain a relevant, authentic network—not manipulate a follower/following ratio.

## Boundaries

- Analyze read-only. Never follow/unfollow, remove followers, block, restrict, report, DM, comment, or like during this workflow.
- Low interaction or missing data does not prove a relationship is absent, fake, or irrelevant. Mark unavailable fields `UNKNOWN`.
- Protect artists, producers, DJs, labels, media, A&Rs, executives, creatives, venues, event organizers, collaborators, recurring contributors, and strategic partners.
- Never infer importance, personal value, or intent from follower counts alone; don't publish person-facing scores.

## Evidence and classification

Use only connector-visible data such as account/category, follow-back status, public metadata, interactions, mentions/tags, collaborations, content relevance, and relationship history. Classify as **CORE RELATIONSHIP**, **ACTIVE COMMUNITY**, **STRATEGIC NETWORK**, **LOW-INTERACTION**, **IRRELEVANT**, **SUSPICIOUS/LOW-QUALITY**, or **UNKNOWN**. Use internal strength **HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/UNKNOWN** only with evidence.

A review candidate needs multiple signals of irrelevance and no known protected relationship or collaboration history. Record account, evidence, relationship/context checks, confidence, reason, and `ACTION: APPROVAL REQUIRED`. Stop when identity, history, or strategic relevance cannot be reliably established.

## Report

Include account balance, following coverage, relationship categories, protected relationships, review candidates, unknown data, and a conservative recommendation. Report counts/differences only when directly observed. Any later action requires separate exact authorization, supported tools, and post-action verification.

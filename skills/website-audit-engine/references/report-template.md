## 6. Remediation plan

Order actions by risk and dependency. Include quick containment, durable fix, validation, and rollback considerations. Mark proposed work as **RECOMMENDATION** until executed and verified.

## 7. Verification and retest plan

List commands, tools, routes, roles, devices, expected outcomes, and evidence to capture for each retest.

## 8. Limitations and unknowns

Record every control that is NOT VERIFIED, why it could not be assessed, and what access or evidence would resolve it.

## 9. Production safety statement

Record whether any production data, configuration, deployment, public submission, or external side effect occurred. If none occurred, say so explicitly.

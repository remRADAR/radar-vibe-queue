# Music Artist Success

> RECONSTRUCTED: Authored during the RADAR operating-skills expansion; the original expansion package was not available. Confirm current distributor/platform rules from authoritative sources at use time.

## Purpose and scope

Support RADAR artist development and release operations: release readiness, track metadata, distribution planning, rights/royalties review, and artist growth strategy. Out of scope: editorial content (radar-editorial-publishing), social analytics (radar-meta).

## Triggers

Music release preparation, ISRC/UPC and metadata, distributor setup, rights and splits, royalties, artist development plans, release calendars.

## Required inputs

Artist identity, release asset list (tracks, artwork, audio files), target platforms/date, existing rights agreements, and current metadata.

## Procedure

1. Inventory the release: tracklist, assets, credits, existing identifiers (ISRC/UPC), agreements.
2. Check metadata completeness and correctness against platform requirements; flag missing/incorrect ISRC, UPC, writer splits, and PRO registration gaps.
3. Verify rights chain: samples cleared, splits documented, agreements consistent with what is claimed.
4. Assess release readiness: assets to spec (formats, loudness/artwork dimensions per current platform rules), metadata, rights — mark each VERIFIED or UNKNOWN.
5. Produce a release-readiness report with blockers, risks, and a dated plan; on authorization and verified tools, execute agreed steps (e.g., distributor uploads) and confirm them.

## Capability and permission checks

Do not assume access to distributor dashboards, PRO portals, or streaming accounts. Verify the tool and authorization before any submission.

## Safety boundaries and approval gates

Submissions to distributors, rights registrations, and any contractual or financial commitment require explicit authorization. Never fabricate ISRCs, release dates, or platform policies.

## Verification

Readiness evidence is per-item: each asset, metadata field, and rights item observed or marked UNKNOWN.

## Failure and escalation

Missing agreements, unclear splits, or unavailable platform data: mark BLOCKED with the exact item and escalate to the artist/user.

## Handoff and continuity

Record release state, blockers, and next actions in the project ledger for cross-agent pickup.

## Completion criteria

Readiness report with evidence delivered, or authorized release steps executed and confirmed.

# Authorized Product Study Queue

**Trigger:** study a website/app/product to inform a user-owned project, authorized audit, or independent functional equivalent.

## Copy-ready prompt

> First establish the user's ownership/permission and the permitted target/scope. If authorization is unclear, limit work to public behavior and user-provided materials. Map only legitimately observable features, user flows, responsive behavior, content organization, and publicly visible integrations. Record evidence and uncertainty; do not infer hidden architecture as fact.
>
> Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, rate limits, or access controls; do not extract private data, credentials, protected source code, or restricted assets. Do not copy proprietary branding, text, or assets without permission. Separate functional inspiration from protected expression and design an independent equivalent where requested.
>
> Deliver a feature/flow map, evidence and confidence, accessibility/usability observations, risks/unknowns, and a scoped implementation plan. If building, use the product/application or web queue and verify the result independently.

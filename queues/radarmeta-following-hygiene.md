# RADARMeta — Following Hygiene & Relationship Review

**Source:** RADARMeta following-hygiene workflow in the supplied Drive skill bundle.

## Copy-ready prompt

> Perform a read-only review of the user-authorized following list only if the active connector exposes the list and enough relationship evidence. Verify account identity, coverage, interaction history, relationship data, and the period reviewed. Mark every unavailable field **UNKNOWN**; do not treat missing interactions as proof that there was no relationship.
>
> Preserve important RADARCharts relationships even when recent engagement is low: artists, producers, DJs, labels, media, A&Rs, executives, creatives, photographers/videographers, venues, event organizers, collaborators, recurring contributors, and strategic partners. Do not infer value or intent from follower counts alone. Do not publish person-facing scores.
>
> Classify only from visible evidence using **CORE RELATIONSHIP**, **ACTIVE COMMUNITY**, **STRATEGIC NETWORK**, **LOW-INTERACTION**, **IRRELEVANT**, **SUSPICIOUS/LOW-QUALITY**, or **UNKNOWN**; use internal relationship strength **HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/UNKNOWN** only when justified. A cleanup candidate requires multiple evidence-backed signs of irrelevance and no known protected relationship; a single weak signal is not enough.
>
> Never unfollow, remove followers, block, restrict, report, DM, comment, like, or follow in this review. For every candidate proposal list account, evidence, relationship/context checks, confidence, reason, and `ACTION: APPROVAL REQUIRED`. If identity, history, or relevance cannot be verified, stop and mark the review unknown.
>
> Report **ACCOUNT BALANCE; FOLLOWING NETWORK; RELATIONSHIP BREAKDOWN; REVIEW CANDIDATES; PROTECTED RELATIONSHIPS; COVERAGE; UNKNOWN DATA; RECOMMENDATION**. Include follower/following counts and their difference only when observed. Do not optimize a ratio by manipulating engagement; actions require a separate explicit approval and supported tool.
